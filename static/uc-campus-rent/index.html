Eight campuses, one question

What does it cost to live near a UC campus?

Thousands of students show up every fall needing a bed, so rent near a campus plays by its own rules. Here's what it costs around eight UC campuses, explained in plain language.

    2New to this?

    Four terms you'll run into

    The ring
    The ZIP codes around a campus where students rent. It starts with every ZIP code whose centre is about 3 miles (5 km) or less from campus, or the three nearest, and is then adjusted by hand: a neighborhood farther out that's full of students is added, and one where students don't rent is left out.
    Median
    The middle value. Sort every rent from cheapest to priciest and take the one halfway down, so a handful of luxury places can't skew the result.
    Fair market rent
    The federal housing department's yearly estimate of what a modest two-bedroom costs in an area, utilities included. Voucher programs use it to set how much rent they cover.
    Forecast range
    A guess about next year's rent, given as a low and a high instead of one number. Rent should land between them about 8 times in 10.

    3The experiment

    Does new campus housing lower rent next door?

    When a university opens a residence hall, hundreds of students leave nearby apartments at once. This site checks whether rent in the neighborhood cools afterward, by comparing it with similar ZIP codes elsewhere in the same metro area.
